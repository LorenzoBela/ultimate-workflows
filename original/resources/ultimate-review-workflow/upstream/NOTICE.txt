The local QA playbook adapts their ideas for host-discovered tooling, observable
state assertions, existing authorization and the Ultimate review verdict.
It replaces unconditional network-idle waits, fixed sleeps, automatic installation
and external-posting or cleanup examples with scoped local requirements.
Exact revisions, source paths and file hashes are in manifest.json.
